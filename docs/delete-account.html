AniNinja — Delete your account

How to request deletion of your AniNinja cloud sync account and its data

How to delete your account

  1. Open AniNinja and go to Settings → Cloud sync → Sign out if you want syncing to stop immediately (optional — deletion works even without this step).
  2. Email nukenoob2010@gmail.com with the subject “AniNinja account deletion request”, using the same Google address you signed in with.
  3. Your request is processed promptly — usually within 48 hours — and you'll receive a confirmation email when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account record, including your email address, display name, avatar URL and Google account identifier
  • Your synced favorites, watch history, playback progress and settings
  • Your usage statistics (for example, total watch time)
  • Your posts and votes on the in-app feedback board

What is kept, and for how long

Data stored only on your device (favorites, history, settings) is never uploaded unless you signed in, and is removed whenever you uninstall the app or clear its data — deleting your cloud account doesn't affect it and it isn't retained on the server.

After a deletion request is completed, nothing associated with your account remains on the sync server. Standard server backups may retain residual data for up to 30 days before being purged.

Notes

  • Deleting your cloud account does not delete the AniNinja app itself or any data stored on your device.
  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone — you can sign in again later, but as a new account with empty sync data.
  • If you can't send email from your signed-in address (for example, you lost access to it), mention the profile name shown in the app so the account can be identified.
